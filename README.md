# Shaware'na (شوارعنا)

A UI/UX design project for the **Shaware'na** app, created in Figma as part of the DEPI Graduation Project.

## Project Overview

**Shaware'na** is a local travel discovery and budgeting app that helps users explore Egypt smartly. It lets users discover budget-friendly destinations, attractions, and local spots, plan personalized trips within a set budget, and find accurate information on prices, local transportation routes, and expenses.

## Project Objectives

- Help users explore Egypt and plan local trips easily while staying within their budget.
- Provide accurate and transparent details about attraction prices, hidden gems, and transportation routes between governorates (e.g., from Cairo to Beheira).
- Deliver an intuitive, engaging, and culturally relevant experience that guides users from discovering a destination to planning the final trip.

## Project Scope

We are designing a platform that helps users discover tourist destinations, cultural spots, and hidden gems across Egypt, with a main focus on routes like Cairo to Beheira and local experiences.

The app brings all the essential travel and budgeting information together in one place. Users can:

- Set a specific budget and see which places they can visit and how to get there.
- Discover budget-friendly destinations and attractions.
- View local transport options, routes, and estimated prices.
- Explore local activities, food spots, and places to stay.
- Save and organize their own custom trips.

## Target Users

- Budget-conscious travelers and students.
- Solo travelers and groups looking for affordable local outings.
- Domestic tourists planning trips between Egyptian governorates.

## Project Plan (5 Weeks)

| Week | Focus | Details |
|------|-------|---------|
| 1 | UX Research & Planning | User interviews, competitor analysis, defining user needs, prioritizing features, user flows, and budgeting logic. |
| 2 | Low- & Mid-Fidelity Wireframes | Wireframes for the main screens, budget calculators, and user journeys. |
| 3 | Design System & High-Fidelity Design | Colors, typography, reusable components, and starting the high-fidelity screens. |
| 4 | High-Fidelity Design & Prototyping | Completing the screens, checking visual consistency, and building an interactive prototype of budget management and navigation. |
| 5 | Usability Testing & Refinement | Testing key use cases with real users, documenting findings, refining the prototype, and preparing the final presentation. |
